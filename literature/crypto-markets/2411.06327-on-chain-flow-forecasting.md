# Return and Volatility Forecasting Using On-Chain Flows in Cryptocurrency Markets

**Authors:** Yeguang Chi, Qionghua Chu, Wenyan Hao
**Venue/Source:** arXiv preprint
**arXiv/DOI:** arXiv:2411.06327
**Date:** November 2024 (revised September 2025)
**Local PDF:** [2411.06327](2411.06327-on-chain-flow-forecasting.pdf)

---

## Core Claim
Hourly USDT net inflow to centralised exchanges positively forecasts BTC and ETH returns at 1-hour frequency — the strongest horizon tested. BTC's own net inflow is *not* predictive for BTC at 1h (only at 4h+), meaning the leading signal is stablecoin purchase intent (external capital entering the ecosystem) rather than coin withdrawals or deposits.

---

## Method
Panel predictive regressions at 1h, 2h, 4h, and 6h frequencies using blockchain-derived exchange net inflow for BTC, ETH, and USDT, covering 2017–2023. Net inflow = coins/tokens sent to exchange-controlled addresses minus coins leaving, sourced from on-chain data (not LOB-derived order flow). Lagged returns included as controls to isolate the flow signal. No trading strategy implemented.

---

## Results
- USDT net inflow to exchanges: statistically significant positive predictor of BTC 1h returns (strongest at 1h, weakens at 4h+).
- ETH own net inflow: negatively forecasts ETH returns at 1h (supply-side pressure channel).
- BTC own net inflow: non-predictive at 1h; borderline at 4h only.
- No trading strategy; no Calmar, Sharpe, or MDD reported. This is a pure forecasting paper.
- Sample period: 2017–2023 (includes both 2021 bull and 2022 deep bear).

---

## Relevance to this project
Directly addresses H14 with a **partial answer**: hourly flow aggregation retains return predictive power in crypto, but via the **stablecoin inflow channel** rather than asset-level order flow. Key distinction:

- The OHLCV-derived NBV proxy `(close - open) / (high - low) × volume` captures *within-exchange* net directional pressure from existing participants.
- This paper's signal is *cross-venue stablecoin inflow* — external USDT capital arriving on-chain before being deployed into BTC. These are complementary, not identical, mechanisms.

**Implication for H14 test:** If the NBV OHLCV proxy fails to show autocorrelation, the reason may be that the H14 hypothesis was implicitly about a different signal (stablecoin inflow) that isn't captured by OHLCV alone. Testing both is warranted before concluding that 1h flow signals don't work.

**Implementation note:** The stablecoin-inflow signal requires CryptoQuant or Glassnode APIs (exchange net flows at hourly resolution). Not available from Freqtrade OHLCV data alone.

Actionable extension: add `usdt_exchange_inflow` as a secondary entry gate on top of the SmaRegime180 slope filter — enter long only when USDT inflow z-score > 0.5 in the prior 1h bar. This combines the regime filter (existing, validated) with a capital-flow filter (new signal). Requires a new data fetch script analogous to `scripts/download_hyperliquid.py`.

**Addresses priority:** P4 — confirms that 1h flow aggregation retains predictive power in crypto (stablecoin inflow channel); the question of whether OHLCV-derived NBV captures the same effect is still open.
