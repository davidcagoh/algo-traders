Four forecasts, six weeks of real BTC price

Each walk-forward origin feeds the last 528 hourly closes into DecisionIntelligence/Aurora and asks for the next 96 hours, price alone, no news or text context yet. The bands are the 10-sample spread (mean ± 1 std) at each hour of the horizon. All four are now scored against what actually happened: skill vs. a flat random-walk baseline is mixed, and the model's own stated uncertainty is badly overconfident — see calibration below.
