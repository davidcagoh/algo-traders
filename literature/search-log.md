# Literature Search Log

Append one dated section per scheduled or manual scan. Record every configured
thread, queries used, candidates screened, selections, access failures, and any
keyword proposal. A no-result scan is useful evidence and must still be logged.

Seed terms in [`search-config.yml`](search-config.yml) are immutable to the
scheduled agent. Candidate terms may be added only under that file's keyword
policy and must include evidence here; only a human-reviewed change may retire a
term.
