Greetings,

Your URL is https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~yourname
e.g. mine is https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~flaps

Put your files under the "public_html" directory under your home directory,
and call your first file index.html.

(Advanced note: "public_html" is actually a symlink pointing to the place the
documents are really supposed to be, which is /cs/htuser/yourname/public_html.
So you can't just make your own such directory.  Please ask me for help
as needed (with this and all other such things!).)

The CS web server serves static files and cgi programs.  Things such as
PHP require you to run your own "user-managed web server"; but if you're
considering going in that direction I'd urge you to consider whether it
adds anything or is just a time sink (and whether the result is
going to be less likely to look right on all web browsers!).

Note that everything under public_html is published.  If you have some
files you don't want to publish (e.g. old versions of files), don't put
them there, or protect them so as not to be readable by others (and
test it).  Don't assume that people won't be able to guess the URLs;
it's easy to guess a suffix of ".old" or a tilde; more complicated things
are still usually pretty easy to guess... just don't publish them if you
don't want them to be published.

Any questions, please don't hesitate to ask.
