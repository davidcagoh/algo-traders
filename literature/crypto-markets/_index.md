# Crypto Markets Literature Index

Authoritative manifest for crypto-market sources and source notes. Coverage was
reconciled through 2026-08-05 using the `literature-scan` discovery convention
and `literature-review` evidence convention.

## Access legend

- **Local** — validated PDF in root `literature/`.
- **Record only** — source is indexed and linked, but no legal local PDF was
  retrievable. The dive records the reason.
- Maturity is stated explicitly because a recent preprint and a peer-reviewed
  article are not equivalent evidence.
